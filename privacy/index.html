Privacy Policy

Cricket with Moosa · Last updated 19 November 2026

What the app is

Cricket with Moosa is a cricket scoring and live-streaming app. You score a match on your phone and can stream it, with the scoreboard, to YouTube and Facebook.

What stays on your phone

Matches, players, scores, voice clips and stream keys are saved only on your device. If you connect YouTube or Facebook, the sign-in is kept on your device (Google keeps its own sign-in; Facebook access is kept in the phone's secure storage). It is never sent to us. We do not run any server that stores your data.

How YouTube and Facebook access is used

YouTube (scope youtube): only to create a live broadcast on your channel when you press Live, connect the video to it, and end it when you stop.

Facebook (public_profile, publish_video, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts): to show your name and the Pages you manage so you can choose where to go live, to create the live video there, and to end it when you stop.

The app does not read your messages, friends, comments or other posts, does not post anything except the live video you start, and does not share or sell any data. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Live score links

When you share a live score link, the match score (team and player names, runs, wickets) is sent through a public message relay so viewers can follow it. Do not put private details in names.

Deleting your data

In the app: open the camera, then "Jagah", then "Hatayein" next to YouTube or Facebook to remove the connection from your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved.

You can also remove the app's access at any time: Google Account → Security → Third-party connections; Facebook → Settings → Apps and websites → Cricket with Moosa → Remove.

We keep no copy of your data on any server, so there is nothing else to delete.
