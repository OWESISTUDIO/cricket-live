What the app is

Cricket with Moosa is an app for scoring cricket matches and streaming them live, with the score on the video, to the user's own YouTube channel or Facebook profile or Page.

Your accounts and your content

When you connect YouTube or Facebook, the app acts only on your instruction: it creates a live video on the account or Page you choose, streams your camera to it and ends it. You are responsible for what you stream and must follow YouTube's and Facebook's own rules. You can disconnect an account at any time in the app's settings.

Public matches

If you show a match to everyone, its team names, score and video links are visible to other app users while it is played. You can turn this off for any match.
